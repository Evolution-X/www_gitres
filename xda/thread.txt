[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=rgb(0, 96, 255)][SIZE=5][B]First Time Install[/B][/SIZE][/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[COLOR=rgb(251, 160, 38)](Note: The latest no longer includes firmware, flash the latest HOS3 FW of your device region first)[/COLOR]
1. Download boot, dtbo, vendor_boot & rom for your device from [URL='https://sourceforge.net/projects/evolution-x/files/marble/15']sourceforge[/URL]
2. Follow the guide at [URL]https://xdaforums.com/t/closed-rom-14-0-marble-official-evolution-x-11-13-24.4640655/post-89539346[/URL]
3 (optional). Reboot to recovery (fully) to sideload any add-ons (e.g magisk)
4. Reboot to system & #KeepEvolving

[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=rgb(0, 96, 255)]Update[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]1. Reboot to recovery
2. While in recovery, navigate to Apply update -> Apply from ADB
3. adb sideload rom.zip (replace "rom" with  actual filename)[/SIZE]
4 (optional). Reboot to recovery to sideload any add-ons (e.g magisk)
[SIZE=3]5. Reboot to system & #KeepEvolving

[COLOR=rgb(0, 96, 255)][SIZE=5][B]OTA[/B][/SIZE][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]1. Check for update. If available, select "Download and install" (approx 10-15 min)
2. Reboot & #KeepEvolving[/SIZE]
[/SPOILER]
